## The question and the answer

User: *"reset-swap is an S3 problem. Can Mamba-3, at related configurations, solve
S5? (description: 60 classes, generated by rotations of order 2 and 3 whose
product has order 5) — which exact configuration, preferably minimal."*

- The description is **A₅** (icosahedral rotations, order 60), not S₅ (order 120).
  Both are built: `--group a5` and `--group s5` (default).
- **A₅: yes, one layer.** Conjugation `v ↦ q v q̄` in a `Rotor4D` 4-block is SO(3).
  Hand-built exact at `reset-swap`'s exact config (`d_model 2`, 2 heads,
  `state_rank 4`, `Trapezoid::None`, no final norm) with a 60-way head — 308 params.
- **S₅: no single layer, any size/rotation kind/mimo/micro_steps.** Proof sketch
  (README "Why this task", test `s5_needs_a_reflection_one_layer_does_not_have`):
  a layer's transition group is a (compact) group of block rotations ⊂ ∏SO(4);
  exact tracking makes S₅ a quotient of it; S₅'s odd elements act on A₅ by the
  outer automorphism (swaps the 72°/144° five-cycles `c` ↔ `s∘c∘s`); conjugation
  in SO(4) preserves both factors' angles, only a reflection swaps them; S₅'s only
  proper quotient is C₂ ⇒ a layer tracks S₅ exactly at most up to its sign.
  (Full argument in this session: the first filtration index where the image
  contains A₅ gives Q ⊂ SO(4) with an A₅ quotient; every such A₅ comes from a 2I in
  an SU(2) factor, N_SU(2)(2I) = 2I, so induced automorphisms are inner.)
- **S₅: yes, two layers** (Krohn–Rhodes cascade `σ = sᵉ∘a`): layer 1 holds the
  sign, layer 2 steps `a` by `c` or `s∘c∘s` depending on it. Hand-built exact at
  `d_model 3`, 3 heads — 902 params — at 96 and 288 symbols.

## Findings worth keeping

1. **Training lands the group on one head at a time.** Every SISO A₅ run had the
   exact icosahedral rotation (180.0°/120.0°, axes 20.9°/69.1°) on ≥1 head, the
   rest a Z₃ or recency trace ⇒ one scalar readout ⇒ not exact. MIMO fixes it:
   `d_model 4, 2 heads, per_head_dim 2, mimo_rank 2` (608 params) trains exact.
   One-head MIMO (`d2 h1 m2`, `d3 h1 m3`) never learned d's half-turn (3/3 runs).
2. **Training often finds A₅'s 4-D irrep**, not conjugation: left/right axes 69.1°
   vs 20.9° (two Galois-twin 2I's), (−1,−1) trivial.
3. **Length leak.** Trained on 32 symbols: exact angles, ᾱ≈0.99 hold ⇒ 56% shuffle
   at 288. Train length 96 prices it in (default now 96).
4. **Hand-built needs a scale-free head**: direction decoder (no bias), `REF_POINT`
   `(5,−5,2)` so the xy-shadow has 60 distinct directions (≥1.58°); S₅ embeds `c`
   as the zero vector so layer 1's decaying sign is a pure direction after the
   pre-norm (otherwise turns drift at 96).
5. **S₅ trainability: open.** Layer 1 learns `c` as a ~72° turn, never the sign.
