## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** burn-mamba follows burn-stack ticket 12. The four types of the caches of
a stack go, and each `Block` impl always gets a cache.

**Current behaviour:**

- `Mamba1Caches`, `Mamba2Caches` and `Mamba3Caches` are structs
  `{ caches: Vec<…> }`. Each has a `CacheStack` impl and a cache traversal that
  forwards to the `Vec`.
- `MambaCaches` is a struct `{ slots: Vec<Option<MambaCache>> }`. Its `from_slots`
  keeps an empty slot, and its cache traversal matches the empty slots.
- The `Block` impls of `Mamba1`, `Mamba2`, `Mamba3` and `MambaBlock` name
  `type Caches`. Their `block_forward`, `block_forward_packed` and `block_step` take
  `Option<…>`.
- The block contract tests run `zero_cache_equals_no_cache` and
  `cache_stack_moves_every_slot`. Their helpers need the bound
  `<C::Block as Block>::Caches: CacheTensors`.
- The bench gives `None` to `block_forward` and `block_step`.
- The examples name `MambaCaches` and call `caches.detach()`.

**Desired behaviour:**

- Each block, each container and each example gives the same values as before.
- The caches of a stack are `Vec<Mamba1Cache>`, `Vec<Mamba2Cache>`, `Vec<Mamba3Cache>`
  or `Vec<MambaCache>`.
- The family API keeps its `Option`. `forward`, `forward_packed` and `step` of
  `Mamba1`, `Mamba2` and `Mamba3` take `Option<…Cache>`. A `None` starts from
  `zero_cache`, as before.

**Key interfaces:**

- **The pin**: both `rev` values of burn-stack in `Cargo.toml` are the pushed commit
  of burn-stack ticket 12 (burn-stack version 0.44.0). The `[patch]` is a comment.
- **These items go**: `Mamba1Caches`, `Mamba2Caches`, `Mamba3Caches` and
  `MambaCaches`, with their `CacheStack` impls, their cache traversals and their
  exports in the preludes and in `lib.rs`.
- **The `Block` impls of `Mamba1`, `Mamba2` and `Mamba3`** have no `type Caches`. Their
  three calls take the cache, and they give `Some(cache)` to the family API.
- **The `Block` impl of `MambaBlock`** has no `type Caches`. Its three calls take a
  `MambaCache` and unwrap the family of the block. A cache of another family still
  panics with "the cache family does not match the block".
- `impl CacheTensors for MambaCache` does not change.
- **The module docs**:
  - The headers of `mamba1::cache`, `mamba2::cache` and `mamba3::cache` describe the
    cache of one layer. They say that the caches of a layer stack are a `Vec` of it.
  - The headers of `family` and `family::adapters` name no `CacheStack`.
- **The tests**:
  - The contract tests `zero_cache_equals_no_cache` and
    `cache_stack_moves_every_slot` go. The bound
    `<C::Block as Block>::Caches: CacheTensors` goes from each helper.
  - `the_flat_caches_keep_empty_slots` goes.
  - `mixed_stack_rules` builds a `Vec<MambaCache>`. Its assertions do not change.
  - `owned_buffers_keep_values_and_assign_moves_them` keeps its stacked form and its
    flat form, on `Vec<Mamba3Cache>` and on `Vec<MambaCache>`.
  - Each test that gives `None` to a `Block` call gives `zero_cache(…)`.
- **The bench**: the runners give a cache to each `Block` call.
  - The forward and train runners build the zero cache inside the timed work. A
    `None` builds it inside the block, so the timed work does not change.
  - The step runner builds the zero cache before the warm-up, which is not timed.
- **The examples**:
  - tiny-stories names `Vec<MambaCache>`. Its `detach_caches` calls
    `cache_detach(caches)`.
  - hnet-stories calls `cache_detach(caches)` on its `HNetCaches`.
  - The docs of the examples name the caches of the network, not `MambaCaches`.

  - ADR-0003: the costs in **Considered Options** name no `from_slots`. The clause
