# 14: The other meaning of "reset" in the tap patterns

Status: needs-triage

Blocked by: None

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

From the triage of ticket 07 (`07-row-end-read.md`). Facts at `c2fb89b2`.

**Problem:** In `../burn-stack/GLOSSARY.md`, a **Reset** is the mark on the first token
of a segment. The tap patterns `TapPattern::HorizontalReset` and
`TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset` use "reset" with another meaning: the lag-1
tap closes at the first micro-step of each token. So one word has two meanings in the
docs of Mamba-3. A packed Mamba-3 call can also use these patterns, so both meanings
can apply to the same call.

**Facts:**

- Both variants are public. A rename breaks the public API.
- The diagram in the docs of `TapPattern` names the two choices of the lag-1 tap "carry
  over" and "reset". The other member of the pair is `HorizontalCarryOver`.
- `GLOSSARY.md` already uses the verb "close" for this meaning. **Tap pattern** says
  "whether the lag-1 tap closes at token boundaries". The _Avoid_ list of **Gate** says
  "a tap that closes at token boundaries".
- The names are in `src/mamba3/tap_pattern.rs` (35 lines) and in 6 other files of
  `src`. Two test names use `horizontal_reset`.
- The note `info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` (3 lines) and its check
  `scripts/mamba-3/trapezoid_as_integration.py` (5 lines) use the variant names. The
  check also has local names such as `nu_reset` and `gam_reset`.
- No example uses the variants. `docs/source-terms.md` does not name them.
- The `reset` ladder of the examples (`examples/reset/`) uses "reset" for a third
  meaning: a symbol of the task that tells the model to forget.

**Possible direction:** Give the variants a name with the verb "close", for example
`HorizontalClosed` and `VerticalPlusHorizontalClosed`. The docs, the note and the check
then follow.

**Questions for the maintainer:**

- Rename the two variants, or keep them and accept the second meaning?
- If you rename them: which word? "Closed" follows the glossary.
- Does the `reset` ladder of the examples stay outside this ticket?
