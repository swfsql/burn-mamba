# 14: "In token", not "reset", in the names of two tap patterns

Status: resolved

Blocked by: None

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

From the triage of ticket 07 (`07-row-end-read.md`). In `../burn-stack/GLOSSARY.md`, a
**Reset** is the mark on the first token of a segment. The tap patterns
`TapPattern::HorizontalReset` and `TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset` use "reset"
with another meaning: the lag-1 tap closes at the first micro-step of each token. A
packed Mamba-3 call can use these patterns, so both meanings can apply to the same call.

## Triage notes

**Decisions of the maintainer:**

- Rename the two variants. The new names end in `InToken`: the lag-1 tap stays inside
  the token. The partner variants `HorizontalCarryOver` and
  `VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver` keep their names.
- The `reset` ladder of the examples (`examples/reset/`) is out of scope. There, "reset"
  is a symbol of the task that tells the model to forget.

**Checks against the code** (at `40af69da`):

- Both variants are public. `TapPattern` derives `serde::Serialize` and
  `serde::Deserialize`, so the rename also changes the serialized names. No saved config
  in `examples/` or `tmp/` uses the two variants. The repo keeps no compatibility with
  saved configs.
- The module of `TapPattern` names the variants on 35 lines. Two test names use
  `horizontal_reset`. Each other "reset" in that module has the tap meaning. Examples are
  the test constant `RESET_AND_TWO_TAP`, the test variable `reset` and assert messages.
  A section comment and the row label `reset (j ≥ 1)` of the lattice diagram also use it.
- Six other files of `src` name the variants. These are the module header of `mamba3`,
  the doc of `helpers::tap_open_mask` and a comment of the double-SSD forward. The tests
  of `packing`, `padding` and `positive` also name them. Each other "reset" in `src`
  means a segment reset.
- The note `info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` names the variants on 3 lines. Its
  check `scripts/mamba-3/trapezoid_as_integration.py` names them on 5 lines. The check
  also uses "reset" for the tap in local names, comments and labels. Examples are
  `en_reset`, `gam_reset`, `nu_reset`, `lam_reset`, `en_reset_u1`, the suffix `_r` of
  `nu_int_r` and `nu_far_r`, and the `LATTICE` keys `"reset-horizontal (…)"`.
- The check gives 74 passed, 0 failed.
- No example, bench, `README.md`, file of `docs/` or entry of `GLOSSARY.md` names the
  variants.
- `GLOSSARY.md` uses the verb "close" for this meaning. **Tap pattern** says "whether the
  lag-1 tap closes at token boundaries".
- The crate version is `0.80.0`. Ticket 15 also breaks the public API.

## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Rename the two tap patterns that use "reset" for a lag-1 tap that closes at
token boundaries. The new names end in `InToken`.

**Current behaviour:**

- The public enum `TapPattern` has the variants `HorizontalReset` and
  `VerticalPlusHorizontalReset`. Under each of them, the lag-1 tap closes at the first
  micro-step of each token.
- The docs and the tests of `TapPattern` use "reset" for these patterns or for their
  tap. The note of the tap lattice and its numpy check also do.
- In the glossaries, a **Reset** is the mark on the first token of a segment. A packed
  Mamba-3 call can use these patterns, so one call can have both meanings.

**Desired behaviour:**

- The two variants have the names in **Names**.
- Each identifier, comment, doc, message and label that uses "reset" for these patterns
  or for their tap changes:
  - For a pattern, use its variant name.
  - For the action of the tap, use the glossary verb "close". An example is "the lag-1
    tap closes at the first micro-step of each token".
- Each "reset" that means a segment reset keeps the word. Do not change a use of "reset"
  only because the word matches.
- The serialized names of the two variants change with them. Add no `#[serde(alias)]`.
- The _Avoid_ line of **Tap pattern** in `GLOSSARY.md` adds "reset (for a lag-1 tap that
  closes at token boundaries)".
- The outputs, the caches and the gradients do not change. No tensor op changes.
- The crate goes up one minor version, in the first commit that breaks the public API.
  That commit message states the break.

**Names:**

- `TapPattern::HorizontalReset` becomes `TapPattern::HorizontalInToken`.
- `TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset` becomes
  `TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken`.
- The test `horizontal_reset_is_none_at_one_micro_step` becomes
  `horizontal_in_token_is_none_at_one_micro_step`.
- The test `horizontal_reset_is_the_carry_over_with_lambda_saturated` becomes
  `horizontal_in_token_is_the_carry_over_with_lambda_saturated`.
- The test constant `RESET_AND_TWO_TAP` becomes `IN_TOKEN_AND_TWO_TAP`. The test
  variable `reset` becomes `in_token`.
- In the lattice diagram of `TapPattern`, the row label `reset (j ≥ 1)` becomes
  `in token (j ≥ 1)`. Keep the columns of the diagram aligned.
- In the numpy check, `reset` in a local name becomes `in_token`. For example,
  `en_reset` becomes `en_in_token`.
- In the numpy check, the suffix `_r` of `nu_int_r` and `nu_far_r` becomes `_i`. The
  suffix `_c` of the carry-over stays.
- In the numpy check, the `LATTICE` keys say "in-token horizontal" in place of
  "reset-horizontal".

**Constraints:**

- Do not change burn-stack. The pin does not move.
- In the tests, change only names, comments and messages. Do not change a check, an
  expected value or a tolerance.
- The numpy check keeps each check, its section numbers and its count. Only names,
  comments and labels change.
