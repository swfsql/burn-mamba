## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Move the burn-stack pin to burn-stack ticket 08, delete the hand-written
backend hop of each cache, implement `Block::zero_cache`, and keep the slot logic of
each family only in `CacheStack`.

**Current behaviour (with the pin of today, after ticket 06):**

- The `CacheStack` impls of `Mamba1Caches`, `Mamba2Caches`, `Mamba3Caches` and
  `MambaCaches` write `cache_to_inner`, `cache_from_inner` and `cache_device` by hand.
  The Mamba-3 impl lists every field for both pathways and both directions. The
  `CacheTensors` impls list the same fields again.
- Each `Block` impl (the three families and `MambaBlock`) has `zero_caches_3d` and
  `zero_caches_2d`. Each one builds `n` caches with the `zero_cache` of its family.
- `Mamba1Caches` and `Mamba2Caches` have inherent `caches_len`, `from_vec`,
  `into_options` and `from_options`, and no code calls them. Their `CacheStack` impls
  write the same logic again.
- `Mamba3Caches` has the same four inherent methods. Its `CacheStack` impl calls them,
  and so does one capture test.
- The test `the_fields_survive_a_no_grad_round_trip` and its doc say that the hop is
  written by hand.

**Desired behaviour:**

- `Cargo.toml` pins burn-stack at the commit of burn-stack ticket 08, or later. Follow
  "The burn-stack pin" in `docs/agents/issue-tracker.md`.
- No cache type of this crate writes a backend hop. The `CacheTensors` traversals are
  the only lists of the cache fields.
- Each `Block::zero_cache` impl of a family calls the `zero_cache` of that family.
  `MambaBlock` tags the cache with its family.
- Each family keeps its slot logic only in its `CacheStack` impl:
  - The eight inherent slot methods of `Mamba1Caches` and `Mamba2Caches` are gone.
  - The bodies of the four inherent slot methods of `Mamba3Caches` move into its
    `CacheStack` impl. The capture test calls `CacheStack`.
  - `Mamba3Caches::double_ssd` and `Mamba3Caches::single_ssd` stay.
- The test `the_fields_survive_a_no_grad_round_trip` stays. Its doc says that the hop
  follows from the traversal.
- The crate goes to the next minor version, in the first breaking commit. The commit
  message states the breaks: the new burn-stack version, `Block::zero_cache`, and the
  deleted inherent slot methods.
