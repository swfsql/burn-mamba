# 06: One traversal per cache, one fresh cache, one tap FIFO

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: `../../block-family-choice/issues/04-block-enum.md`

From the architecture review of 2026-11-03. Line numbers are those of commit
`42ca3d0e`. Not compiled.

**Files:** `src/unified/cache.rs:308-383`, `src/unified/capture.rs:37-168`,
`src/mamba3/double_ssd/cache.rs:108-165,249-297`, `src/mamba3/single_ssd/cache.rs:108-152,234-282`,
`src/mamba3/cache.rs:189-213`, `../burn-stack/src/modules/cache.rs:36-102,138-173`.

**Problem:** One more cached tensor costs about 20 edits that carry no knowledge.
The Mamba-3 fields have 5 lists, and 12 struct literals name all 6 fields. A fresh
cache has 2 sources, and 2 config structs hold the same 12 fields. `tropical_bh` has
37 non-test mentions in 8 files. A field missing from `cache_to_inner` shows only
when a cut crosses a config that holds this field.

The tap slots are two `Option`s, "present or absent together". 2 asserts and 10
`.expect` calls hold that rule.

**Direction, in steps:**

1. One fresh-cache constructor and one cache config. This step is the strongest.
2. The `CacheTensors` traversal becomes the one hand-written list. `cache_to_inner`,
   `cache_from_inner` and `cache_device` follow from it. Zero caches take the batch
   and the device.
3. One tap FIFO value, optional as a whole. It owns its slot order and its zeros.
4. One field group inside both Mamba-3 cache types.

Split step 2 by repo at triage.

**Holds:** ADR-0003: the list stays by hand, and both cache types stay.

**Cost:** `CacheTensors` becomes mandatory. A map through the visitor costs a
refcount per tensor per cut, and no tensor op. A nested `#[derive(Module)]` group is
not compiled. 39 test lines name the tap fields.

### Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** One fresh-cache constructor per family (Part 1). One `TapFifo` value for the
two tap tensors of Mamba-3 (Part 2). One frame cache inside both Mamba-3 cache types
(Part 3, an experiment).

**Current behaviour:**

- **Fresh caches:**
  - Each family has a `…CacheConfig` and a `…CachesConfig`. Mamba-3 has both for each
    pathway, so there are 8 types. Each `…CacheConfig` copies the shape fields of the
    block (12 for Mamba-3), with `new_from_block_config` and `init`.
  - `forward` and `step` of each family also build a fresh cache inline when the caller
    gives none. Mamba-2 also builds the origin of a packed call this way. Mamba-3 builds
    it in `Mamba3::zero_cache` and in the `fresh` closure of `plant_entry`.
  - The `Block` adapters build their zero caches through the configs. The bench helper
    `double_ssd_cache` builds a double-SSD cache from a `Mamba3Config`.
- **Tap tensors:** both Mamba-3 cache types have `k_state_bumhr: Option<Tensor<5>>` and
  `v_state_buhp: Option<Tensor<4>>`. They are present or absent together. Two asserts and
  10 `.expect` calls hold that rule. `Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` and `save_tap_slots` return
  a pair of `Option`s. The forward of each pathway reads them, and `save_tap_slots` reads
  the incoming FIFO and the FIFO of the origin.
- **Mamba-3 fields:** both cache types have the same 6 fields: `ssm_bhpr`, the two tap
  tensors, `rotation`, `log_precision_bh` and `tropical_bh`. These places list all of
  them:
  - the two struct definitions, the two `From` impls and the two `sanity` methods,
  - the `CacheTensors` traversal, the hop in the `Block` adapters and the fresh caches,
  - `TestCache` in the packing tests.

  Only `ssm_bhpr` has a different meaning in the two types (ADR-0003). The plant frame
  writes the other fields.

**Desired behaviour, Part 1 (one fresh-cache constructor, required):**

- Each family has one constructor: `Mamba1::zero_cache(batch, device) -> Mamba1Cache`,
  `Mamba2::zero_cache(batch, device) -> Mamba2Cache` and
  `Mamba3::zero_cache(batch, device) -> Mamba3SingleSsdCache`.
- A caller that needs a fresh double-SSD cache converts it with `.into()`. A fresh cache
  is at a call boundary, so the move is lossless (ADR-0003).
- Each fresh cache of the crate comes from these constructors. This includes:
  - the inline builders of `forward` and `step`,
  - the origin of Mamba-2,
  - the `fresh` closure of the plant frame,
  - the `Block` adapters, which call `zero_cache` once for each slot,
  - the bench helper, which builds the block from its config.
- The 8 cache config types are gone, with `new_from_block_config` and their prelude
  re-exports.
- The crate goes from 0.76.0 to 0.77.0 in this commit. The commit message states two
  breaks:
  - the deleted cache configs,
  - the break of ticket 05, which had no bump: the 5 deleted marker traits, and the SSD
    ops that take `OpRestarts` and `OpSeeds`.

**Desired behaviour, Part 2 (one `TapFifo`, required):**

- `TapFifo` holds the tap FIFO (`GLOSSARY.md`), oldest first:
  - `b_bumhr`, shape `[batch, tap_slots, mimo_rank, nheads, state_rank]`,
  - `x_buhp`, shape `[batch, tap_slots, nheads, per_head_dim]`.
- Both Mamba-3 cache types have `tap_fifo: Option<TapFifo>`. It is `None` under
  `TapPattern::None`.
- `TapFifo` owns its layout:
  - its zeros, its `sanity` and its part of the `CacheTensors` traversal,
  - the docs of the slot order and of the decay pre-scale of `x`.
- `Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` and `save_tap_slots` return `Option<TapFifo>`. Each reader
  takes one `TapFifo`. No code checks that two tap tensors are both present or both
  absent.
- The masses and the tap terms stay in each pathway (ADR-0007). The row-end read inside
  `save_tap_slots` (`window`, `last_origin`) does not change, because ticket 07 owns it.
- The hand-written hop in the `Block` adapters follows the new field. Ticket 11 deletes
  it.
- Every doc that names `k_state_bumhr` or `v_state_buhp` names the new identifiers.
  - The "Tap cache" row of `docs/source-terms.md` names `TapFifo`, `b_bumhr` and `x_buhp`.
    `k_state` and `v_state` stay in that file only as the words of the official code.
  - A doc that says "tap slots" for the whole FIFO says "tap FIFO". "Slot" stays for one
    position.

**Desired behaviour, Part 3 (one frame cache, an experiment):**

Try these forms in this order. Keep the first one that meets the conditions below.

1. **A field group:** `Mamba3FrameCache` holds the frame cache (`GLOSSARY.md`):
   `tap_fifo`, `rotation`, `log_precision_bh` and `tropical_bh`. It derives `Module`, as
   the caches do today.
   - Both Mamba-3 cache types have `ssm_bhpr` and `frame: Mamba3FrameCache`.
   - The `From` impls move these two fields. The frame cache has one `sanity` and one
     traversal, and both cache types use them.
   - Test lines change from `cache.tropical_bh` to `cache.frame.tropical_bh`. The
     maintainer accepts that change.
2. **A generic struct**, if the nested `#[derive(Module)]` does not compile:
   - One struct, generic over a pathway marker, replaces the two cache structs.
     `Mamba3DoubleSsdCache` and `Mamba3SingleSsdCache` are the aliases of its two
     instantiations. They stay distinct types, so a chunked pass cannot mix them
     (ADR-0003).
   - The `From` impls become one re-tag. The doc of `ssm_bhpr` gives both meanings: the
     state and the accumulator.
   - If `#[derive(Module)]` rejects the marker, and no code needs `Module` on a Mamba-3
     cache, the struct derives only `Clone` and `Debug`.
   - `Mamba3FrameCache` does not exist. The **Frame cache** term stays in
     `GLOSSARY.md`, because it names the fields that the plant frame writes.
3. **A revert**, if form 2 needs a change in Burn or in burn-stack. Write the failed
   condition in this ticket, with the short form of the compiler error. Both outcomes
   meet this brief.

Keep a form only if all of these conditions hold:

- `cargo check` and `cargo doc --all --no-deps` give no warnings.
- All tests pass, with no change to an expected value or a tolerance.
- Burn and burn-stack do not change.

The plant frame keeps the double-SSD cache type in its signature in all three forms.

**Also in this ticket:** `TestCache::map` in the packing tests calls
`CacheTensors::zip_tensors`, because a map is a zip with the clone. `TestCache::visit`
stays.
