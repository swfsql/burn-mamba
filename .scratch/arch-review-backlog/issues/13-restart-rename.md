## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Rename each use of "restart" that means a reset, or the state that a
reset chunk starts from, with the terms of the glossaries.

**Current behaviour:**

- The public types `packing::Restarts` and `packing::OpRestarts` hold the resets of a
  packed call, as an SSD pass takes them. The SSD input structs have a `restarts`
  field. The SSD ops and kernels have a `restarts` parameter.
- `restart_heads` and `Segments::restart_heads` put the values of the origin at the
  first positions of each reset chunk.
- The SSD kernels and their backward call the state that a reset chunk starts from its
  "restart state" (`origin + seedᶜ`, or `origin`). Their local names also use the word.
  Examples are `restart_bn`, `restart_factor`, `d_restart_bnhpr`, `d_restart_vec_bhpr`
  and `restart_bhpr`.
- Comments say "a chunk that restarts" for a chunk with a reset.

**Desired behaviour:**

- Each use of "restart" that means a reset, the start state or the origin heads has a
  name from **Names**. This includes identifiers, comments and messages.
- A use with another meaning keeps the word. The report of this ticket lists it.
- The outputs, the caches and the gradients do not change. No tensor op changes.
- The crate goes up one minor version, in the first commit that breaks the public API.
  That commit message states the break.

**The meanings of "restart":**

Before you rename a use of "restart", find its meaning:

- **A reset**: the mark at the first chunk of a segment, or a chunk with that mark.
  Rename it with "reset".
- **The start state**: the state that such a chunk starts from (`origin + seedᶜ`, or
  `origin`). Rename it with "start state".
- **The origin values at the heads of a reset chunk** (`restart_heads`). Rename it with
  "origin heads".
- **Another meaning**: keep the word. List the use (item and sentence) in the report
  of this ticket, for the maintainer.

Do not rename a use of "restart" only because the word matches.

**Names:**

- `packing::Restarts` becomes `packing::SsdResets`.
- `packing::OpRestarts` becomes `packing::OpResets`.
- The `restarts` fields and parameters become `resets`.
- `restart_heads` and `Segments::restart_heads` become `origin_heads` and
  `Segments::origin_heads`. The name of the test
  `restart_heads_gradient_sums_over_the_chunks` follows.
- The "restart state" of a chunk becomes its "start state".
- The local names of the SSD kernels and of their backward get "reset" or "start"
  names, by their meaning.

**Constraints:**

- Do not change burn-stack. The pin does not move.
- Change no test, except the name of the test above. Do not change an expected value
  or a tolerance.
