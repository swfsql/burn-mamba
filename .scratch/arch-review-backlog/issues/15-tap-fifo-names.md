# 15: "Tap FIFO", not "tap slots", in the names of the FIFO methods

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: None

> *This was generated by AI after the implementation of ticket 07.*

Ticket 06 (`06-cache-field-lists.md`) and ticket 07 (`07-row-end-read.md`) kept the names
`Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` and `Mamba3::save_tap_slots`, because their briefs named them.
Each method gives the whole tap FIFO. `GLOSSARY.md` puts "tap slots (for the whole FIFO)"
under the _Avoid_ list of **Tap FIFO**. This ticket renames the two methods.

The maintainer decided that this work is a ticket of its own, not a part of ticket 13 or
ticket 14. Ticket 13 renames another word ("restart"). Ticket 14 waits for triage.

## Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Rename the two Mamba-3 methods that give the whole tap FIFO, with the
glossary term **Tap FIFO**.

**Checks against the code** (at `1bd24255`):

- `Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` is public. It gives the tap FIFO of a fresh cache, or `None`
  under a pattern with no `β` tap. The fresh frame cache of `src/mamba3/cache.rs` is its
  one caller.
- `Mamba3::save_tap_slots` is `pub(crate)`. It gives the tap FIFO for the next call. The
  entry of the plant frame is its one caller.
- No test, example, bench, doc outside `src` or note in `info/` names the two methods.
- `TapPattern::tap_slots` gives the number of slots of the FIFO. The shape docs of
  `TapFifo` use `tap_slots` for that number. The glossary permits this use: "One slot
  holds one write position."
- The crate version is `0.79.0`. Ticket 13 also breaks the public API, and each breaking
  ticket bumps the minor version (`CLAUDE.md`).

**Current behaviour:**

- The public method `Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` and the crate method `Mamba3::save_tap_slots`
  use "tap slots" for the whole FIFO.

**Desired behaviour:**

- The two methods have the names in **Names**. Their docs and the comments at their call
  sites say "tap FIFO" for the whole FIFO.
- A use of "slot" for one position of the FIFO stays. `TapPattern::tap_slots` and the
  shape name `tap_slots` stay.
- The outputs, the caches and the gradients do not change. No tensor op changes.
- The crate goes up one minor version, in the first commit that breaks the public API.
  That commit message states the break.

**Names:**

- `Mamba3::zero_tap_slots` becomes `Mamba3::zero_tap_fifo`. It follows the other fresh
  fields of `Mamba3`: `zero_cache`, `zero_rotation_state`, `zero_positive_state`.
- `Mamba3::save_tap_slots` becomes `Mamba3::save_tap_fifo`.

**Constraints:**

- Do not change burn-stack. The pin does not move.
- Change no test. Do not change an expected value or a tolerance.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] `rg 'zero_tap_slots|save_tap_slots' src examples benches docs info README.md GLOSSARY.md CLAUDE.md`
      finds nothing.
- [ ] `TapPattern::tap_slots` keeps its name.
- [ ] `./scripts/check.sh` passes.
- [ ] `cargo test --lib -- --test-threads=1` gives 0 failed, and the same count of passed
      tests as before the work.
- [ ] The version in `Cargo.toml` is one minor version above the version at the start of
      the work.

**Benches:** none. No tensor op changes.

**Out of scope:**

- `TapPattern::tap_slots` and the shape name `tap_slots`: they count slots.
- The word "restart": ticket 13.
- `TapPattern::HorizontalReset`: ticket 14.
- The docs and the code of burn-stack.
