# 10: One standard SSD scan on primitives for Mamba-2 and double-SSD

Status: resolved

Blocked by: None

From candidate 3 of the architecture review (`tmp/arch-review/batch-b1.md`) and the
triage of ticket 04.

**Problem:** The standard SSD scan exists twice on primitives. Mamba-2 holds the scan
as its five kernels. The Mamba-3 copy has the same structure and two more axes:
`mimo_rank` and the read axis. The double-SSD pathway calls that copy once for each term
of the trapezoid. A K4 or reset fix needs a second derivation. The batching of
`e43b32e3` reached only the Mamba-3 copy, so Mamba-2 still walks K5 chunk by chunk.

### Triage notes

**Decisions of the maintainer:**

- The standard SSD scan starts in Mamba-2, as one SSD call. Mamba-3 based double-SSD on
  that scan because of the trapezoid: one SSD call for each term. The single-SSD pathway
  came after double-SSD.
- This step comes before ticket 04, or in the same change. Triage selected "before",
  for the reasons below. One session can still do both tickets in order.
- `./bench.sh` and `./kernels.sh` already cover Mamba-2.
- The maintainer accepted a code read for the peak memory of ticket 04. Triage applies
  the same gate here. The maintainer did not decide this ticket separately.

**Checks against the code:**

- A throwaway probe compared the public functions of the crate, in
  `tmp/triage-04/probe/`:
  - One side is the Mamba-2 forward and `combined_backward`.
  - The other side is one SSD call of the double-SSD code, reduced to the Mamba-2 case.
    That case has `m = 1`, `u = 1`, `v = dt·x` and `da = dt·A`. The side adds the chain
    rule and the `D` skip.
  - On `flex` at fp32, the worst relative difference is 7.1e-7. The probe covers 10
    cases (5 shapes, each with and without resets), `y`, the final state and all 8
    gradients. A control showed that the comparison sees a 1% error.
- So this ticket needs no new math.
- `src/mamba2/` cannot call `src/mamba3/`. Each family is a separate feature, and
  `scripts/check.sh` builds each family alone. The Mamba-3 copy of the scan needs a home
  outside `src/mamba3/`.
- `mimo_rank` and the read axis are not the reason for double-SSD. The single-SSD
  pathway has both axes. `mimo_rank` is in the first Mamba-3 commit (`2bc33821`), before
  the trapezoid (`69ed6310`).
- The shared helpers of ticket 04 go to the same home. In the other order, those
  helpers move twice.
- This order also keeps the time gate clean: the `mamba1` and `mamba3` rows show the
  noise of the run.
- The peak memory by reading:
  - The K5 working set grows from one chunk to half of the chunks.
    `Mamba3SsdPath::backward_chunk_group` gives the bound: about six score-shaped
    tensors at half width stay under about four at full width in the forward K5.
  - The forward K5 of Mamba-2 also runs at full width.
  - `e43b32e3` measured this change on Mamba-3: 1847 MiB as a walk, 1685 MiB in halves.
  - The shared function holds `v` and `decayed_v`, where Mamba-2 holds `b_scaled`. These
    tensors are much smaller than a score tensor.
- `oracle_da_local_matches_einsum_minus_ddt_dt` reads two test-only fields. At the
  level of `v`, the identity is `d_da_local = Σₚ out·d_y − Σₚ d_v_orange·v`, because
  `d_dt_orange·dt = Σₚ d_v_orange·x·dt`. The probe did not run this identity.

### Agent brief

**Category:** enhancement

**Summary:** Mamba-2 and double-SSD call one standard SSD scan on primitives. That scan
has the axes of the Mamba-3 copy: `mimo_rank` and the read axis. Mamba-2 keeps only its
chain rule and its `D` skip.

**Current behaviour:**

- Mamba-2 holds the standard SSD scan on primitives: K1–K5, the extended K3 and the
  analytic backward.
- That backward walks K5 and K4 chunk by chunk.
- The `mamba3` module holds a copy of that scan, with `mimo_rank` and the read axis. A
  build with only the `mamba2` feature does not compile that module.

**Desired behaviour:**

1. One module outside the three family modules holds the standard SSD scan on primitives,
   with `mimo_rank` and the read axis. It compiles when `mamba2` or `mamba3` is on. It holds:
   - the forward K1–K5 and `k3_ssd_chunk_state_extended`
   - `k4_ssd_state_passing_backward` and `cat_chunk_groups`
   - `combined_backward` with `CombinedGrads`
   - the `prim` read-axis helpers, `chunk_tokens` and `backward_chunk_group`
2. Double-SSD calls that module. Its operations and their order do not change.
3. The forward of Mamba-2 forms `v_bnlhp = dt·x` and `da_bnlh = dt·a_decay`, calls the
   module with a length-1 `m` axis and `read_stride = 1`, and adds `D·x`.
4. The backward of Mamba-2 calls the shared `combined_backward` with the same inputs,
   then applies the chain rule:
   - `d_x = d_v·dt + d_y·D`
   - `d_dt = Σₚ d_v·x + d_da·a_decay`
   - `d_a_decay = Σ_{b,n,l} d_da·dt`
   - `d_d = Σ_{b,n,l,p} d_y·x`
   - `d_b`, `d_c`, `d_initial_state` and `d_origin` are the values of the shared
     function, with no `m` axis.
5. The outputs, the final caches and the gradients of every family and SSD path stay
   the same.

**Key interfaces:**

- `Mamba2BackendExt` and the `RecomputeOp` of Mamba-2 keep their inputs.
- `Mamba3DoubleSsdBackendExt` keeps its signature.
- The two `CombinedGrads` of Mamba-2 and double-SSD stay separate types.

**Constraints:**

- The `mamba2` feature does not imply `mamba3`.
- The module stays in this crate (ADR-0002).
- The docs of the module name the standard SSD scan and its two callers. They do not
  describe Mamba-2 as a case of double-SSD.
- The name of the module does not use "core". `GLOSSARY.md` lists "SSD core" under
  `_Avoid_`. A new term goes to `GLOSSARY.md` through `/domain-modeling`.
- A function takes each large tensor by value. A caller clones a tensor only if the
  caller reads that tensor again.
- The length-1 `m` axis exists only at the call into the module.
- `Minimal` and `Serial` of Mamba-2 do not change. They are the check of ADR-0007.
- `oracle_da_local_matches_einsum_minus_ddt_dt` stays, with the identity at the level
  of `v`. Its test-only fields move to the shared `CombinedGrads`.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [x] `src/mamba2/` holds no primitive K1–K5 and no reverse per-chunk loop.
- [x] The `combined_backward` of Mamba-2 holds only the forming of `v` and `da`, the
      call, the chain rule and the `D` skip.
- [x] `cargo test --lib -- --test-threads=1` passes with no change to an expected value
      or a tolerance, and the oracle test runs.
- [x] A temporary mutation of each line of the chain rule fails at least one Mamba-2
      test. Each mutation is reverted.
- [x] `cargo test --examples -j 1 -- --test-threads=1` and `./scripts/check.sh` pass.
      `./scripts/check.sh` builds each family alone.
- [x] `cargo check` and `cargo doc --all --no-deps` give no warnings.
- [x] The module headers of Mamba-2 do not say that this path mirrors `ssd_combined.py`.
- [x] An ADR records the trade-off: the fast form of Mamba-2 does not mirror
      `_mamba_chunk_scan_combined_bwd` line by line.
- [x] The File Map of `CLAUDE.md` names the new module.

**Benches:** The agent stops after the last commit, because only the user runs the
benches. The reports in the repo before the first commit of this ticket are the
baseline.

- The launch count of `train` `mamba2` in the `cuda` column of `kernels.md` does not
  rise.
- The forward of Mamba-2 changes its operations, so the `forward` `mamba2` count can
  change. The user judges that change.
- The counts of `mamba1` and of every `mamba3` row in the `cuda` column do not change.
- The time of `mamba2` stays within run noise or improves, as the user judges it.
- The peak memory has no measurement. The maintainer accepted a code read on ticket 04,
  and the notes above give the reading for this ticket.

**Out of scope:**

- The shared K1–K3 and BLUE backwards of ticket 04.
- The single-SSD pathway.
- `step`, `Minimal` and `Serial` of every family.
