### Choices of the sweep

- **History phrases in records.** A record of a fix gives the old state and
  the current state. For example, container-tests ticket 02 says that the
  module header "said that each setting changes one value. The fixed header
  does not say it."
- **A quote of a banned phrase.** In place of the quote, the text names the
  commit that holds the phrase:
  - arch-review ticket 23: the old verb of the `expect` message is in this
    ticket at `bf81d7cd`,
  - the spec: the example of a past passive is the comment of the zero-pad
    columns of `src/mamba2/mamba2.rs:955` at `9a8958ec`.
- **A quote of current code.** Arch-review ticket 13 quoted the comment of
  three `summ.py` files, and the comment has a semicolon. The record gives the
  line of the comment and its word "restarts".
- **The minor version.** Arch-review tickets 13, 14 and 15 say that the first
  commit that breaks the public API "bumps the minor version of the crate".
  These are the words of `CLAUDE.md`.
- **One verb for one action.** Each file uses one of `delete` and `remove`:
  - arch-review ticket 01 and container-tests ticket 04 take `delete`, the
    verb of most of their uses,
  - arch-review ticket 20 takes `delete`, the word of the coverage report for
    a changed line,
  - arch-review ticket 24 takes `remove`, the verb of its other two uses,
  - third-party-terms ticket 04 takes `delete`, the verb of its instruction in
    the body,
  - in arch-review ticket 02, the optional groups of the in-projection
    "separate" from the end. That sentence names a split, not a deletion.
- **Motion verbs.** The phrasal verbs of motion become `decreases`, `returns`
  or `bumps`. The last item of glossary ticket 01 says "The work found no new
  term and no new decision".
- **Lists.** Twelve long sentences become lists. Examples are the uses of
  "Step", "Slot" and "Row" in glossary ticket 04, and the summary of
  arch-review ticket 11.
- **Paragraphs.** Six long paragraphs split by topic. In arch-review ticket 19,
  the proposed glossary text becomes a block quote. In third-party-terms
  ticket 04, the answer to question 1 gets its own paragraph.
- **Active voice.** A passive with a clear actor becomes active. Examples are
  "This work amends ADR-0007", "This ticket blocks ticket 07", "The change
  deletes …" and "`## Comments` lists …".
- No term of the two glossaries changes.
