1//! # The trapezoid's tap pattern — which earlier sample the `β` tap reads
2//!
3//! Mamba-3's write is a two-tap filter on the state input: the current sample
4//! at `γₜ = λₜΔₜ` and an earlier one at `βₜ = (1−λₜ)Δₜαₜ`, transported across
5//! the gap between them (`helpers::trapezoidal_coefficients`). At
6//! [`micro_steps`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::micro_steps) `= 1`
7//! "earlier" can only mean the previous token and there is nothing to choose;
8//! at `u > 1` the folded sequence carries `u` positions per token
9//! ([`crate::mamba3::product`]) and the choice is real.
10//!
11//! [`Trapezoid`] names the members of that lattice.
12//! `info/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §§8–9 derives it, prices each member and
13//! proves the invariant they all keep (each tap transported across *its own*
14//! gap, which is what preserves the single-SSD `Δ̃` collapse) — cite it, it is
15//! not restated here.
16//!
17//! ## One rule for every member
18//!
19//! A pattern says which earlier samples are **admissible** at each folded
20//! position; what they are *worth* is not a second thing to decide. The step's
21//! mass `Δₚ` is split by two learned per-(head, micro-step) scalars, and a tap
22//! that is not admissible hands its mass back one level — interior → far,
23//! far → `γ`:
24//!
25//! ```text
26//!   γ = λΔ            the right endpoint, what the read sees   (λ = σ(λ̂))
27//!   ν = (1−λ)Δ        the left-endpoint mass                   (μ = σ(μ̂))
28//!   νⁱⁿᵗ = ν·μ        at the lag-1 tap  — pairs micro-steps inside a token
29//!   νᶠᵃʳ = ν − νⁱⁿᵗ   at the lag-`u` tap — the same micro-step of the previous token
30//! ```
31//!
32//! so `γ + νⁱⁿᵗ + νᶠᵃʳ = Δ` at every position, whatever is gated. `λ` keeps the
33//! meaning the note gives it (the splitting parameter of §4); `μ` — the one
34//! channel the two-tap members add — says **which earlier sample the left
35//! endpoint is**, interpolating between the two candidates. The quadrature
36//! weights therefore stay non-negative and still sum to `Δ`, so §5's
37//! "top-up, never a rollback" reading survives the wider tap set, and so does
38//! the collapse: sample `s` carries the one scalar `γₛ + νⁱⁿᵗₛ₊₁ + νᶠᵃʳₛ₊ᵤ`.
39//!
40//! The fallback is what makes the gated members *submodels* rather than
41//! different animals: [`HorizontalReset`](Trapezoid::HorizontalReset) is
42//! [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Trapezoid::HorizontalCarryOver) with `λ = 1` at each
43//! token's first micro-step (§8's own limit), and
44//! [`VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver`](Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver)
45//! *contains* both implemented single-tap patterns at `μ ≡ 1` and `μ ≡ 0`.
46//! Dropping the mass instead of handing it back would make both statements
47//! false and would quietly weaken the write at `1/u` of the positions.
48
49/// Which earlier sample(s) the trapezoid's `β` tap reads.
50///
51/// The choice is **structural**, not a knob on one common algorithm: it decides
52/// the shift(s) the chunkwise pathways apply before chunking, the width of the
53/// single-SSD γ-correction band, whether the in-projection spends `λ` and `μ`
54/// channels at all, and how many `(B, x)` tap slots the cache carries
55/// ([`tap_slots`](Self::tap_slots)).
56///
57/// The lattice is the product of the two taps' settings — the lag-1
58/// (*horizontal*) one being absent, gated to within a token, or unrestricted,
59/// times the lag-`u` (*vertical*) one being absent or present — and all six
60/// cells exist:
61///
62/// ```text
63///                   no vertical                 + vertical (lag u)
64///   no horizontal   None                        Vertical
65///   reset (j ≥ 1)   HorizontalReset             VerticalPlusHorizontalReset
66///   carry (all j)   HorizontalCarryOver         VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
67/// ```
68///
69/// The left column is one algorithm read at two lags
70/// ([`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag)) — the lag-`u` cell of that column *is*
71/// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) — and the right column adds one lag-1 tap to
72/// it, mixed in by `μ` (module header). At `u = 1` the column collapses: every
73/// row's two taps coincide, so [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) *is*
74/// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver), the two-tap members are
75/// it as well (they fold, and spend no `μ` channels —
76/// [`has_interior_tap`](Self::has_interior_tap)), and
77/// [`HorizontalReset`](Self::HorizontalReset) *is* [`None`](Self::None).
78#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Default, serde::Serialize, serde::Deserialize)]
79pub enum Trapezoid {
80    /// **No second tap.** `λ ≡ 1`, hence `β = 0` and `γ = Δ`: the write is plain
81    /// exponential-Euler, i.e. Mamba-2's (the note's §4 Lie–Trotter row).
82    ///
83    /// Structural in the same sense as
84    /// [`RotationKind::Real1D`](crate::mamba3::rotation::RotationKind::Real1D),
85    /// and nothing is paid for the absent term: the in-projection spends no `λ`
86    /// channels (so Muon sees no `λ` segment), no `β` is formed, the caches'
87    /// tap slots are `None`, `forward` makes **one** SSD call, and `step` drops
88    /// the second outer product. The two SSD pathways coincide here — with no second
89    /// pass to fuse, single-SSD's composite key scale is `γ` and its
90    /// same-step correction is the whole diagonal — so
91    /// [`forward_single_ssd`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3::forward_single_ssd)
92    /// runs the double-SSD code and the caches convert by field identity at
93    /// *every* position, not just at boundaries.
94    None,
95
96    /// **Lag `u`, always**: the tap reads the *same* micro-step of the previous
97    /// **token**, so every tap crosses a token boundary and the pattern is `u`
98    /// parallel token-rate filters, one per micro-step channel. Restores the
99    /// `u = 1` tap semantics at every micro-step.
100    ///
101    /// What is "vertical" is the **tap graph**, not the scan order: the state
102    /// still runs the one flattened chain
103    /// ([`crate::mamba3::product`]), which is what keeps the pattern causal and
104    /// `forward` equal to an unrolled `step`. A scan that truly took every token
105    /// at micro-step `0` before micro-step `1` would have to read token `t+1`
106    /// before finishing token `t` — or keep `u` separate states, which is a
107    /// layer stack (`burn_stack::Layers`), not a tap pattern.
108    ///
109    /// At token resolution the pattern has a closed form: with `Aᵗ` the token
110    /// transition and `ṽᵗⱼ` micro-step `j`'s write transported to the end of its
111    /// own token, the tap's transport `M₍ₜ₋₁,ⱼ₊₁₎:₍ₜ,ⱼ₎` factors through the
112    /// state's own, and the whole `β` side lands **at the token boundary**:
113    ///
114    /// ```text
115    ///   hₜ = Aₜ · ( hₜ₋₁ + Σⱼ νₜ,ⱼ ṽₜ₋₁,ⱼ ) + Σⱼ γₜ,ⱼ ṽₜ,ⱼ ,     ν = (1−λ)Δ
116    /// ```
117    ///
118    /// i.e. **the `u = 1` trapezoid at token resolution, its left endpoint
119    /// promoted from rank 1 to rank `u`** — where
120    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) taps only the previous
121    /// token's *last* micro-step. `A` is untouched, so it is still `λ`-free
122    /// (`info/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §7).
123    ///
124    /// Costs (§9): key scale `γₛ + (1−λₛ₊ᵤ)Δₛ₊ᵤ`; the tap is transported across
125    /// its own `u`-position gap; the cache's tap buffer becomes a `u`-deep FIFO
126    /// (exactly the previous token). The single-SSD same-step correction widens
127    /// from the diagonal to a `u`-wide band — and that band is **exactly the
128    /// token** at the only positions whose output survives (`j = u−1`), so it is
129    /// one small intra-token contraction outside the chunked kernel rather than
130    /// a wider mask (`crate::mamba3::single_ssd::token_band`).
131    Vertical,
132
133    /// **Lag 1, suppressed at each token's first micro-step**: taps pair
134    /// micro-steps *within* a token and never cross a token boundary. Having no
135    /// cross-token path at all, it cannot do the job the trapezoid was
136    /// introduced for — it is a component of a pattern rather than an
137    /// alternative to one.
138    ///
139    /// The suppressed step has no admissible earlier sample, so its whole mass
140    /// returns to `γ` (`λ = 1` there, module header): each token **starts** on
141    /// plain exponential-Euler and is trapezoidal inside. That is exactly the
142    /// limit `info/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §8 shows is reachable by
143    /// learning under [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver), which
144    /// makes this member that pattern's constrained **submodel** rather than a
145    /// rival to it — and is why the mass is handed back rather than dropped.
146    ///
147    /// Caches like [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver): the same
148    /// one-slot lag-1 buffer, ignored at each token's first micro-step. (That is
149    /// what makes the carried value inert across a call boundary — the slot is
150    /// layout, not information.)
151    HorizontalReset,
152
153    /// **Lag 1, always** — the default: one
154    /// tap per position of the folded sequence, so `1/u` of the taps cross a
155    /// token boundary and `(u−1)/u` pair two projections of the same token
156    /// (§8). The cache carries one `(B, x)` slot, the last micro-step of the
157    /// last token.
158    #[default]
159    HorizontalCarryOver,
160
161    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) **and**
162    /// [`HorizontalReset`](Self::HorizontalReset) at once, the left-endpoint
163    /// mass shared between them by `μ` instead of the two jobs sharing one `λ`.
164    /// The first of the two members whose tap graph is not a single lag on the
165    /// folded chain: on the `token × micro-step` grid, cell `(t, j)` is written
166    /// by *two* taps — `(t−1, j)` above it and `(t, j−1)` beside it, the latter
167    /// only for `j ≥ 1`.
168    ///
169    /// What is 2-D is that graph, not the algorithm. Both taps are transported
170    /// across their own gap (`1` and `u` positions of the same folded chain), so
171    /// §9's collapse still applies term by term: the state stays one matrix and
172    /// sample `s` still carries **one** scalar, `γₛ + νⁱⁿᵗₛ₊₁ + νᶠᵃʳₛ₊ᵤ`, into
173    /// every later step, hence one single-SSD pass. Costs over
174    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical): one more per-micro-step scalar channel
175    /// (`μ`) and a third nonzero per row of the mask's banded factor — the band
176    /// itself is already `u` wide, and the within-token tap never crosses a
177    /// token boundary, so the cache and the boundary seed are
178    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical)'s unchanged. On the double-SSD pathway the
179    /// two taps have different shifts and cannot share a pass, so that pathway
180    /// runs **three** SSD calls; this pattern's home is the single one.
181    VerticalPlusHorizontalReset,
182
183    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) **and**
184    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) at once — the same two
185    /// taps as [`VerticalPlusHorizontalReset`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset)
186    /// with the lag-1 one left unrestricted, so at `j = 0` it reads the previous
187    /// token's *last* micro-step while the lag-`u` tap reads its *first*. Both
188    /// candidates for the step's left endpoint are then always available, and
189    /// `μ` interpolates between them everywhere.
190    ///
191    /// Hence the member that **contains** the two single-tap patterns rather
192    /// than sitting beside them: `μ ≡ 1` is
193    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) and `μ ≡ 0` is
194    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical), both exactly, and `μ` is per (head,
195    /// micro-step) — so the choice the rest of this enum makes at configuration
196    /// time is made here by descent instead.
197    ///
198    /// Costs over [`VerticalPlusHorizontalReset`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset):
199    /// nothing in the cache (the `u`-deep FIFO's **newest** slot *is* the lag-1
200    /// slot, and it carries the empty decay product), and one extra term in the
201    /// single-SSD boundary seed — the lag-1 tap now crosses a call boundary,
202    /// which under the reset it never does.
203    VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver,
204}

206impl Trapezoid {
207    /// Whether the pattern has a second (`β`) tap at all — `false` only for
208    /// [`None`](Self::None). The predicate every site branches on: no tap means
209    /// no `λ` channels, no `β` coefficient, no tap slots in the cache, and no
210    /// previous-sample term anywhere in the recurrence.
211    pub fn has_beta_tap(self) -> bool {
212        self != Trapezoid::None
213    }
214
215    /// How far back the `β` tap reaches, in **folded** positions
216    /// ([`crate::mamba3::product`]): `0` with no tap, `1` for the lag-1
217    /// patterns, `u` for the lag-`u` ones.
218    ///
219    /// This is the crate's single knob for the two implemented tapping
220    /// patterns — [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) is `lag =
221    /// 1` and [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) is `lag = u`, and every site that
222    /// touches the tap (the double-SSD shift, the single-SSD key scale, `step`'s
223    /// FIFO depth, the cache) reads it rather than branching on the pattern. At
224    /// `u = 1` they return the same `1`, which is why the two coincide there.
225    ///
226    /// The tap must be transported across *its own* gap, so a lag-`L` tap
227    /// carries `Πᵈ⁼⁰..ᴸ⁻¹ αₚ₋ᵈ` rather than `αₚ`; that is the condition
228    /// `info/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §9 shows preserves the `Δ̃` collapse,
229    /// hence the single-SSD pathway.
230    pub fn tap_lag(self, micro_steps: usize) -> usize {
231        match self {
232            Trapezoid::None => 0,
233            Trapezoid::HorizontalCarryOver | Trapezoid::HorizontalReset => 1,
234            Trapezoid::Vertical
235            | Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset
236            | Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver => micro_steps,
237        }
238    }
239
240    /// Whether the pattern adds a **second**, lag-1 tap beside the one at
241    /// [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag) — the two-tap members, and only while the two
242    /// lags differ.
243    ///
244    /// At `u = 1` they do not: both taps read the same sample, so the split is
245    /// a decomposition of one coefficient and the pair **folds** back into a
246    /// single tap. The fold is structural, as
247    /// [`None`](Self::None)'s missing `λ` is — no `μ` channels in the
248    /// in-projection, no `μ` segment for Muon, one shift, one SSD pass — which
249    /// is what makes a two-tap member at `u = 1` *be*
250    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) rather than an
251    /// expensive spelling of it.
252    pub fn has_interior_tap(self, micro_steps: usize) -> bool {
253        micro_steps > 1
254            && matches!(
255                self,
256                Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset
257                    | Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
258            )
259    }
260
261    /// Whether the tap at [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag) is admissible at a token's
262    /// **first** micro-step, where it is the only tap that would cross a token
263    /// boundary. `false` for [`HorizontalReset`](Self::HorizontalReset) alone,
264    /// whose left-endpoint mass then returns to `γ` (module header).
265    pub fn far_tap_crosses_tokens(self) -> bool {
266        self != Trapezoid::HorizontalReset
267    }
268
269    /// Whether the *interior* (lag-1) tap of a two-tap member is admissible at a
270    /// token's first micro-step, where it would cross a token boundary — the
271    /// only difference between
272    /// [`VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver)
273    /// (`true`) and
274    /// [`VerticalPlusHorizontalReset`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalReset)
275    /// (`false`). Where it is closed, `μ`'s share returns to the far tap.
276    ///
277    /// Only consulted when [`has_interior_tap`](Self::has_interior_tap).
278    pub fn interior_tap_crosses_tokens(self) -> bool {
279        self == Trapezoid::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
280    }
281
282    /// How many `(B, x)` tap slots a cache carries for this pattern at
283    /// `micro_steps` — the depth of the FIFO the tap reads from, hence
284    /// [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag) exactly: a lag-`L` tap needs the last `L`
285    /// positions live. The SSM state is one matrix whatever this says — only the
286    /// trapezoid's tap buffer changes.
287    ///
288    /// A slot is a *layout*, not a claim that something crosses the call
289    /// boundary through it: [`HorizontalReset`](Self::HorizontalReset) shares
290    /// the carry-over's slot and ignores it per token. Nor does a second tap
291    /// need a second buffer — the `u`-deep FIFO's **newest** slot *is* the
292    /// lag-1 slot, carrying the empty decay product, so the two-tap members read
293    /// one FIFO twice.
294    pub fn tap_slots(self, micro_steps: usize) -> usize {
295        self.tap_lag(micro_steps)
296    }
297}

299/// Everything the discretisation needs from the block: the tap pattern, the
300/// micro-steps its gates are periodic in, and the two clamps the coefficients
301/// are formed under.
302///
303/// Carried by [`Mamba3`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3)

316    /// `Δ`'s clamp
317    /// ([`Mamba3Config::dt_limit`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::dt_limit)).
318    pub dt_limit: (f64, f64),
319    /// `A`'s floor
320    /// ([`Mamba3Config::a_floor`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::a_floor)).
321    pub a_floor: f64,
322}

357    /// Unroll `step` over the sequence, from a fresh cache.
358    fn unrolled(model: &Mamba3, input_bsm: &Tensor<3>) -> (Tensor<3>, Mamba3Cache) {
359        let tokens = input_bsm.dims()[1];
360        let mut cache: Option<Mamba3Cache> = None;
361        let mut outs = Vec::new();
362        for t in 0..tokens {
363            let (o, c) = model.step(input_bsm.clone().narrow(1, t, 1).squeeze_dim(1), cache);
364            outs.push(o.unsqueeze_dim::<3>(1));
365            cache = Some(c);
366        }
367        (Tensor::cat(outs, 1), cache.expect("a non-empty sequence"))
368    }

581        let mut carry = config.clone().init(&device);

1089    /// Append `width` dead columns (zero weights, constant bias) to a block's
1090    /// in-projection — how a wider pattern is built from a narrower one's
1091    /// weights, so the two differ in nothing but the appended segment.
1092    fn append_dead_segment(
1093        block: &Mamba3,
1094        into: &mut Mamba3,
1095        width: usize,
1096        bias: f64,
1097        device: &burn::prelude::Device,
1098    ) {
1099        use burn::module::Param;
1100        let w = block.in_proj.weight.val();
1101        let [d_model, _] = w.dims();
1102        into.in_proj.weight = Param::from_tensor(Tensor::cat(
1103            vec![w, Tensor::zeros([d_model, width], device)],
1104            1,
1105        ));
1106        let b = block.in_proj.bias.as_ref().expect("has_proj_bias").val();
1107        into.in_proj.bias = Some(Param::from_tensor(Tensor::cat(
1108            vec![b, Tensor::full([width], bias, device)],
1109            0,
1110        )));
1111    }
