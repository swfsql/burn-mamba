1//! # The tap pattern of the trapezoid — which earlier sample the `β` tap reads
2//!
3//! The write of Mamba-3 is a two-tap filter on the samples: the current
4//! sample at `γₜ = λₜΔₜ`, and an earlier one at `βₜ = (1−λₜ)Δₜαₜ`, transported
5//! across the gap between them (`helpers::trapezoidal_coefficients`). At
6//! [`micro_steps`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::micro_steps) `= 1`,
7//! "earlier" can only mean the previous token, so there is no choice. At
8//! `u > 1`, the write axis has `u` positions per token
9//! ([`crate::mamba3::product`]), and the choice is real.
10//!
11//! [`TapPattern`] names the members of that lattice.
12//! `info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §§8–9 derives it, prices each
13//! member, and proves the invariant that all members keep: each tap is
14//! transported across *its own* gap, which keeps the single-SSD `Δ̃` collapse.
15//! This module cites the note and does not restate it.
16//!
17//! ## One rule for every member
18//!
19//! A pattern says which earlier samples are **admissible** at each write
20//! position. Their *weight* is not a second decision. Two learned per-(head,
21//! micro-step) scalars split the mass `Δₚ` of the micro-step. A tap that is not
22//! admissible gives its mass back one level. The lag-1 tap of a two-tap
23//! member gives it to the lag-`u` tap. The tap at
24//! [`tap_lag`](TapPattern::tap_lag) gives it to `γ`. Under
25//! [`HorizontalInToken`](TapPattern::HorizontalInToken), that tap is a lag-1 tap.
26//! The masses are:
27//!
28//! ```text
29//!   γ = λΔ            the right endpoint, what the read sees   (λ = σ(λ̂))
30//!   ν = (1−λ)Δ        the left-endpoint mass                   (μ = σ(μ̂))
31//!   νⁱⁿᵗ = ν·μ        at the lag-1 tap  — pairs micro-steps inside a token
32//!   νᶠᵃʳ = ν − νⁱⁿᵗ   at the lag-`u` tap — the same micro-step of the previous token
33//! ```
34//!
35//! So `γ + νⁱⁿᵗ + νᶠᵃʳ = Δ` at every position, open or closed. `λ` keeps the
36//! meaning that the note gives it (the splitting parameter of §4). `μ`, the
37//! one channel that the two-tap members add, says **which earlier sample is
38//! the left endpoint**: it interpolates between the two candidates. Thus the
39//! quadrature weights stay non-negative and still sum to `Δ`. The "top-up,
40//! never a rollback" reading of §5 holds for the wider tap set, and so does the
41//! collapse: sample `s` carries the one scalar `γₛ + νⁱⁿᵗₛ₊₁ + νᶠᵃʳₛ₊ᵤ`.
42//!
43//! Because of this fallback, the members whose lag-1 tap closes at token
44//! boundaries, and the two-tap members, are *submodels*, not different models:
45//!
46//! - [`HorizontalInToken`](TapPattern::HorizontalInToken) is
47//!   [`HorizontalCarryOver`](TapPattern::HorizontalCarryOver) with `λ = 1` at
48//!   the first micro-step of each token (the limit of §8).
49//! - [`VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver`](TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver)
50//!   *contains* both implemented single-tap patterns, at `μ ≡ 1` and `μ ≡ 0`.
51//!
52//! If a closed tap dropped its mass instead of giving it back, both statements
53//! would be false, and the write would silently be weaker at `1/u` of the
54//! positions.
55
56/// Which earlier sample(s) the `β` tap of the trapezoid reads.
57///
58/// The choice is **structural**, not a knob on one common algorithm. It
59/// decides:
60///
61/// - the shift(s) that the chunkwise pathways apply before chunking,
62/// - the width of the single-SSD γ-correction band,
63/// - whether the in-projection has `λ` and `μ` channels,
64/// - how many slots the tap FIFO of the cache holds ([`tap_slots`](Self::tap_slots)).
65///
66/// The lattice is the product of the settings of the two taps. The lag-1 tap
67/// is absent, closed at token boundaries, or unrestricted (the `Horizontal…`
68/// part of a name). The lag-`u` tap is absent or present (the `Vertical`
69/// part). All six cells exist:
70///
71/// ```text
72///                      no lag-u tap                + lag-u tap
73///   no lag-1 tap       None                        Vertical
74///   in token (j ≥ 1)   HorizontalInToken           VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken
75///   carry (all j)      HorizontalCarryOver         VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
76/// ```
77///
78/// The single-tap members are one algorithm at two lags
79/// ([`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag)). The right column adds one lag-1 tap, mixed in
80/// by `μ` (module header). At `u = 1` the lags are equal, so:
81///
82/// - [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) *is*
83///   [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver),
84/// - the two-tap members are also `HorizontalCarryOver` (they fold and have no
85///   `μ` channels, see [`has_two_taps`](Self::has_two_taps)),
86/// - [`HorizontalInToken`](Self::HorizontalInToken) *is* [`None`](Self::None).
87#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Default, serde::Serialize, serde::Deserialize)]
88pub enum TapPattern {
89    /// **No second tap.** `λ ≡ 1`, so `β = 0` and `γ = Δ`: the write is plain
90    /// exponential-Euler, as in Mamba-2 (the §4 Lie–Trotter row of the note).
91    ///
92    /// Structural, as
93    /// [`RotationKind::Real1D`](crate::mamba3::rotation::RotationKind::Real1D)
94    /// is. The absent term costs nothing:
95    ///
96    /// - the in-projection has no `λ` channels (so Muon sees no `λ`
97    ///   sub-projection),
98    /// - no `β` is formed, and the tap FIFO of each cache is `None`,
99    /// - `forward` makes **one** SSD call, and `step` has no second outer
100    ///   product.
101    ///
102    /// The two SSD pathways are the same here. With no second pass to fuse, the
103    /// composite key scale of single-SSD is `γ`, and its γ-correction is the
104    /// whole diagonal. So
105    /// [`forward_single_ssd`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3::forward_single_ssd)
106    /// runs the double-SSD code, and the caches convert by field identity at
107    /// *every* position, not only at boundaries.
108    None,
109
110    /// **Lag `u`, always**: the tap reads the *same* micro-step of the previous
111    /// **token**. So every tap crosses a token boundary, and the pattern is `u`
112    /// parallel token-rate filters, one per micro-step channel. It restores the
113    /// `u = 1` tap semantics at every micro-step.
114    ///
115    /// The **tap graph** is "vertical", not the scan order. The state still
116    /// runs the one flattened chain ([`crate::mamba3::product`]), which keeps
117    /// the pattern causal and `forward` equal to an unrolled `step`. A scan
118    /// that really took every token at micro-step `0` before micro-step `1`
119    /// would have to read token `t+1` before it finished token `t`. The
120    /// alternative, `u` separate states, is a layer stack (`burn_stack::Layers`),
121    /// not a tap pattern.
122    ///
123    /// At token resolution the pattern has a closed form. Let `Aᵗ` be the
124    /// token transition and `ṽᵗⱼ` the write of micro-step `j`, transported to
125    /// the end of its own token. The transport of the tap,
126    /// `M₍ₜ₋₁,ⱼ₊₁₎:₍ₜ,ⱼ₎`, factors through that of the state, and the whole
127    /// `β` side lands **at the token boundary**:
128    ///
129    /// ```text
130    ///   hₜ = Aₜ · ( hₜ₋₁ + Σⱼ νₜ,ⱼ ṽₜ₋₁,ⱼ ) + Σⱼ γₜ,ⱼ ṽₜ,ⱼ ,     ν = (1−λ)Δ
131    /// ```
132    ///
133    /// This is **the `u = 1` trapezoid at token resolution, with its left
134    /// endpoint promoted from rank 1 to rank `u`**.
135    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) taps only the *last*
136    /// micro-step of the previous token. `A` is unchanged, so it is still
137    /// `λ`-free (`info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §7).
138    ///
139    /// Costs (§9):
140    ///
141    /// - the key scale is `γₛ + (1−λₛ₊ᵤ)Δₛ₊ᵤ`,
142    /// - the tap is transported across its own `u`-position gap,
143    /// - the tap buffer of the cache becomes a `u`-deep FIFO (exactly the
144    ///   previous token),
145    /// - the single-SSD γ-correction widens from the diagonal to a
146    ///   `u`-wide band. At the only positions whose output survives
147    ///   (`j = u−1`), that band is **exactly the token**. So it is one small
148    ///   intra-token contraction outside the chunked kernel, not a wider mask
149    ///   (`crate::mamba3::single_ssd::token_band`).
150    ///
151    /// **Under a Kalman [`Gain`](crate::mamba3::positive::Gain) at `u > 1`**,
152    /// this tap has no exact filter form. Its installment arrives `u`
153    /// micro-steps late, after `u − 1` predicts that a filter would have made
154    /// with it. The gain counts it as a lag-1 installment, so `Λ`
155    /// over-estimates the weight that the plant wrote, and `η/Λ` is only
156    /// approximately its weighted mean.
157    /// The block still runs, and its pathways still agree. The lag-1 patterns
158    /// are exact ([`crate::mamba3::positive::kalman`]).
159    Vertical,
160
161    /// **Lag 1, closed at the first micro-step of each token**: taps pair
162    /// micro-steps *within* a token and never cross a token boundary. With no
163    /// cross-token path, it cannot do the job that the trapezoid exists for. It
164    /// is a component of a pattern, not an alternative to one.
165    ///
166    /// The closed micro-step has no admissible earlier sample, so all its mass
167    /// returns to `γ` (`λ = 1` there, module header). Each token **starts** on
168    /// plain exponential-Euler and is trapezoidal inside. This is exactly the
169    /// limit that `info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §8 shows learning
170    /// can reach under [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver). So
171    /// this member is a constrained **submodel** of that pattern, not a rival.
172    /// That is also why the mass goes back instead of being dropped.
173    ///
174    /// Caches like [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver): the same
175    /// one-slot lag-1 buffer, ignored at the first micro-step of each token.
176    /// So the carried value has no effect across a call boundary: the slot is
177    /// layout, not information.
178    HorizontalInToken,
179
180    /// **Lag 1, always** (the default): one tap per position of the write
181    /// axis. So `1/u` of the taps cross a token boundary, and `(u−1)/u`
182    /// pair two projections of the same token (§8). The cache holds one
183    /// `(B, x)` slot: the last micro-step of the last token.
184    #[default]
185    HorizontalCarryOver,
186
187    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) **and**
188    /// [`HorizontalInToken`](Self::HorizontalInToken) at once. `μ` shares the
189    /// left-endpoint mass between them, so the two jobs do not share one `λ`.
190    /// Its tap graph is not a single lag on the write axis. On the
191    /// `token × micro-step` grid, *two* taps write cell `(t, j)`: `(t−1, j)`
192    /// above it, and `(t, j−1)` beside it (for `j ≥ 1` only).
193    ///
194    /// Only the graph is 2-D, not the algorithm. Each tap is transported
195    /// across its own gap (`1` and `u` positions of the same write axis), so
196    /// the collapse of §9 still applies term by term. The state stays one
197    /// matrix, and sample `s` still carries **one** scalar,
198    /// `γₛ + νⁱⁿᵗₛ₊₁ + νᶠᵃʳₛ₊ᵤ`, into every later micro-step. So single-SSD still
199    /// needs one pass.
200    ///
201    /// Costs over [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical):
202    ///
203    /// - one more per-micro-step scalar channel (`μ`),
204    /// - a third nonzero per row of the banded factor of the mask.
205    ///
206    /// The band is already `u` wide, and the within-token tap never crosses a
207    /// token boundary. So the cache and the boundary seed are those of
208    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical). On the double-SSD pathway, the two taps
209    /// have different shifts and cannot share a pass, so that pathway runs
210    /// **three** SSD calls. The single pathway suits this pattern better.
211    ///
212    /// Under a Kalman [`Gain`](crate::mamba3::positive::Gain) at `u > 1`, it is
213    /// inexact through its lag-`u` tap, as [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) is.
214    VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken,
215
216    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) **and**
217    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) at once. It has the
218    /// same two taps as
219    /// [`VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken), with
220    /// the lag-1 tap unrestricted. So at `j = 0`, the lag-1 tap reads the
221    /// *last* micro-step of the previous token, and the lag-`u` tap reads its
222    /// *first*. Both candidates for the left endpoint of the micro-step are always
223    /// available, and `μ` interpolates between them everywhere.
224    ///
225    /// So this member **contains** the two single-tap patterns: `μ ≡ 1` is
226    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) and `μ ≡ 0` is
227    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical), both exactly. `μ` is per (head,
228    /// micro-step), so descent makes here the choice that the rest of this enum
229    /// makes at configuration time.
230    ///
231    /// Costs over [`VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken):
232    ///
233    /// - nothing in the cache: the **newest** slot of the `u`-deep FIFO *is*
234    ///   the lag-1 slot, with the empty decay product,
235    /// - one extra term in the single-SSD boundary seed: here the lag-1 tap
236    ///   crosses a call boundary. Under
237    ///   [`VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken),
238    ///   it never crosses one.
239    ///
240    /// Under a Kalman [`Gain`](crate::mamba3::positive::Gain) at `u > 1`, it is
241    /// inexact through its lag-`u` tap, as [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) is.
242    VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver,
243}
244
245impl TapPattern {
246    /// Whether the pattern has a second (`β`) tap. `false` only for
247    /// [`None`](Self::None). Every site branches on this predicate: no tap
248    /// means no `λ` channels, no `β` coefficient, no tap FIFO in the cache,
249    /// and no previous-sample term in the recurrence.
250    pub fn has_beta_tap(self) -> bool {
251        self != TapPattern::None
252    }
253
254    /// How far back the `β` tap reaches, in **write** positions
255    /// ([`crate::mamba3::product`]): `0` with no tap, `1` for the lag-1
256    /// patterns, `u` for the lag-`u` ones.
257    ///
258    /// This is the one knob for the single-tap patterns:
259    /// [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver) is `lag = 1` and
260    /// [`Vertical`](Self::Vertical) is `lag = u`. Every site that touches the
261    /// tap (the double-SSD shift, the single-SSD key scale, the FIFO depth of
262    /// `step`, the cache) reads it instead of branching on the pattern. At
263    /// `u = 1` both return `1`, which is why the two are equal there.
264    ///
265    /// The tap must be transported across *its own* gap, so a lag-`L` tap
266    /// carries `Πᵈ⁼⁰..ᴸ⁻¹ αₚ₋ᵈ`, not `αₚ`.
267    /// `info/mamba-3/trapezoid-as-integration.md` §9 shows that this condition
268    /// keeps the `Δ̃` collapse, and thus the single-SSD pathway.
269    pub fn tap_lag(self, micro_steps: usize) -> usize {
270        match self {
271            TapPattern::None => 0,
272            TapPattern::HorizontalCarryOver | TapPattern::HorizontalInToken => 1,
273            TapPattern::Vertical
274            | TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken
275            | TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver => micro_steps,
276        }
277    }
278
279    /// Whether the pattern has **two** taps at `micro_steps`: a lag-1 tap beside
280    /// the lag-`u` tap at [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag). True for the two-tap
281    /// members, and only when the two lags are different.
282    ///
283    /// At `u = 1` the lags are equal: both taps read the same sample, so the
284    /// split is a decomposition of one coefficient, and the pair **folds** back
285    /// into one tap. The fold is structural, as the missing `λ` of
286    /// [`None`](Self::None) is: no `μ` channels in the in-projection, no `μ`
287    /// sub-projection for Muon, one shift, one SSD pass. Thus a two-tap member
288    /// at `u = 1` *is* [`HorizontalCarryOver`](Self::HorizontalCarryOver), not
289    /// an expensive form of it.
290    pub fn has_two_taps(self, micro_steps: usize) -> bool {
291        micro_steps > 1
292            && matches!(
293                self,
294                TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken
295                    | TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
296            )
297    }
298
299    /// Whether the tap at [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag) is admissible at the
300    /// **first** micro-step of a token, where it would cross a token boundary.
301    /// `false` only for
302    /// [`HorizontalInToken`](Self::HorizontalInToken), whose left-endpoint mass
303    /// then returns to `γ` (module header).
304    pub fn tap_crosses_tokens(self) -> bool {
305        self != TapPattern::HorizontalInToken
306    }
307
308    /// Whether the lag-1 tap of a two-tap member is admissible at the first
309    /// micro-step of a token, where it would cross a token boundary. This is the
310    /// only difference between
311    /// [`VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver)
312    /// (`true`) and
313    /// [`VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken`](Self::VerticalPlusHorizontalInToken)
314    /// (`false`). Where it is closed, the share of `μ` returns to the lag-`u`
315    /// tap.
316    ///
317    /// Read only when [`has_two_taps`](Self::has_two_taps).
318    pub fn two_tap_lag1_crosses_tokens(self) -> bool {
319        self == TapPattern::VerticalPlusHorizontalCarryOver
320    }
321
322    /// The number of slots in the tap FIFO of a cache, for this pattern at
323    /// `micro_steps`. It is the depth of the FIFO that the tap reads, so
324    /// exactly [`tap_lag`](Self::tap_lag). A lag-`L` tap needs the last `L`
325    /// positions.
326    /// The state is always one matrix. Only the tap FIFO changes.
327    ///
328    /// A slot is a *layout*. It does not mean that something crosses the call
329    /// boundary through it: [`HorizontalInToken`](Self::HorizontalInToken) shares
330    /// the slot of the carry-over and ignores it per token. Also, a second tap
331    /// needs no second buffer. The **newest** slot of the `u`-deep FIFO *is*
332    /// the lag-1 slot, with the empty decay product, so the two-tap members
333    /// read one FIFO twice.
334    pub fn tap_slots(self, micro_steps: usize) -> usize {
335        self.tap_lag(micro_steps)
336    }
337}

339/// Everything the discretisation needs from the block: the tap pattern, the
340/// micro-steps (the period of the token boundaries, where a tap can close), and
341/// the two clamps on the coefficients.
342///
343/// [`Mamba3::tap_pattern_spec`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3::tap_pattern_spec)
344/// makes it, and `helpers::trapezoidal_coefficients` takes it. So `forward`
345/// and `step` compute the masses from **one** definition. The same reason
346/// makes [`RotationSpec`](crate::mamba3::rotation::RotationSpec) exist.
347#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq)]
348pub struct TapPatternSpec {
349    /// Which earlier sample(s) the `β` tap reads ([`TapPattern`]).
350    pub pattern: TapPattern,
351    /// Recurrence micro-steps per token (`u`). A tap that closes at token
352    /// boundaries closes with this period. At `u = 1`, a two-tap pattern folds
353    /// ([`TapPattern::has_two_taps`]).
354    pub micro_steps: usize,
355    /// `Δ`'s clamp
356    /// ([`Mamba3Config::dt_limit`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::dt_limit)).
357    pub dt_limit: (f64, f64),
358    /// `A`'s floor
359    /// ([`Mamba3Config::a_floor`](crate::mamba3::mamba3::Mamba3Config::a_floor)).
360    pub a_floor: f64,
361}
