Backward of k3_ssd_chunk_state_extended.

Forward, on the fused length, with k for the input projection of K3: decay = exp(cumA_last − cumA), decayed_v = decay · v and intra_state = decayed_v @ k.

k_bnLMhr has the fused write axis. A pathway that scales B gives the scaled tensor and splits the returned gradient itself.
