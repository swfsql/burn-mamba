Expand description

Backward of K5: BLUE and the intra-chunk term, one chunk group at a time.

Neither term is recurrent. Each needs only the slices of its chunk and the input state of that chunk, which the recomputed K4 gave batched. The group size sets the memory of the score [b, group, h, TM, LM] against the peak of the forward (see backward_chunk_group). On single-SSD, the state of this function is the accumulator of that pathway.

The intra-chunk term serves both K5 forwards:

w[TM, LM]    = cb[TM, LM] · decay[TM, LM] · scale[LM]
intra[TM, p] = Σ_LM w[TM, LM] · v[LM, p]
  • ORANGE: scale_bnlh is None and mask_diagonal is 1. The sum includes the same position.
  • LOWER: scale_bnlh is Some and mask_diagonal is 0. The sum excludes the same position.

Without a scale, the statements below form no tensor for it. prod is w, and d_prod is d_w.

§Arguments

  • d_y_bnhTMp: the gradient of the terms, on the read axis
  • v_bnlmhp, c_bntmhr, cb_bnhTMLM, chunk_input_state_bnhpr: the K5 inputs
  • da_cumsum_bhnLM, da_cumsum_bhnTM: see k5_da_cumsum_on_chunk_axes
  • scale_bnlh: the scale per source position, or None
  • mask_diagonal: the diagonal of read_causal_mask
  • read_stride: write positions per read position

§Returns

The gradients of the two terms, and d_scale_bhnl: the intra-chunk part of d_scale, or None without a scale.
