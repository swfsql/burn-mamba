The standard SSD scan on primitives: the forward and the analytic backward that Mamba-2 and the double-SSD pathway of Mamba-3 call.

§The standard SSD scan on primitives

One SSD call computes the scan of one recurrence with a scalar decay, chunk by chunk:

hₜ = exp(daₜ)·hₜ₋₁ + Bₜ vₜᵀ
yₜ = Cₜ hₜ

v is the input after its scale, and da is the log-decay Δ·A. This module holds the forward K1–K5 and the analytic backward of that call. They use backend primitives, so that a recompute node can run them.

The standard SSD scan has two callers, in their SerialRecalculated paths:

  • Mamba-2 makes one SSD call for each call of its block. It forms v = Δ·x and da = Δ·A, and it adds the D skip and the chain rule back to its own inputs (crate::mamba2::ssd::serial_recalculated).
  • The double-SSD pathway of Mamba-3 makes one SSD call for each term of the trapezoid (crate::mamba3::double_ssd::ssd::serial_recalculated).

The single-SSD pathway makes one SSD call for all terms, with its own forward K5 and its own diagonal term. It assembles its backward from the backwards of K1–K5 in kernels.rs, and it uses K1–K4 and the helpers of this module.

The scan has the two axes that the callers need: mimo_rank and the read axis. A caller without such an axis passes a length-1 m axis and read_stride = 1. Each math statement of the scan is in the header of src/mamba2/mamba2.rs or src/mamba3/mamba3.rs. The decision to keep one scan is ADR-0010.

This module compiles when the mamba2 or the mamba3 feature is on.
