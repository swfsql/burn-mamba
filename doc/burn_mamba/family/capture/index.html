burn_mamba::family

Module capture 

Source
Expand description

Each family’s CacheTensors traversal: a captured step writing its new cache in place. Captured-step support for the caches: the CacheTensors traversal of each family. With it, a captured step (CapturedStep) writes its new cache into the buffers of the old one.

There is one traversal per cache type, and it visits every tensor field. An optional field must be present in both caches or in neither. This module also shows why the stable cache must first be copied into buffers of its own (into_owned_buffers): the Mamba-3 tap slots come back from step as narrows of its own tensors.
