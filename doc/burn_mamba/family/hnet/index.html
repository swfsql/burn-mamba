Runtime-selectable H-Net language models (MambaHNetVocabNet): one family for the stages, one for the main stack. Runtime-selectable H-Net language models: MambaHNetVocabNet and its serializable MambaHNetVocabNetConfig.

The H-Net itself is block-generic and lives in burn_stack::modules::hnet. Its encoders and decoders take one block family E, and its main stack takes another family M (HNetVocabNetwork<E, M>). This module wraps each enabled pair (E, M) of Mamba families in one enum variant. So a caller can choose both at runtime and serialize the choice. A cache or an SSD path of the wrong family panics, as in the other enums of crate::family.
