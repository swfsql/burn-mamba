§norm_f: Option<RmsNorm>

Optional final RMSNorm before Self::out_proj: the counterpart of VocabNetwork::norm_f, which is unconditional there.

It makes the input of the head scale-free. This matters whenever the output magnitude of the stack is not O(1). A plain additive residual grows it with depth. Residuals::MultiGate is a convex mixture (mean-pooled over n streams) that shrinks it. Without the norm, the two schemes give out_proj signals of very different scale.
