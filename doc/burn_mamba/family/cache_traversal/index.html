The cache traversal of each family: the one list of the tensors of each cache type. The cache traversal of each family: the CacheTensors impl of each cache type. It is the one list of the tensors of its cache type. [burn_stack::modules::cache] gives the operations that follow from it. Two examples are the backend hop of an untracked span and the cache write-back of a captured step (CapturedStep). A captured step writes its new cache into the buffers of the old one.

An optional field must be present in both caches or in neither.

This module also shows why the stable cache must first be copied into buffers of its own (into_owned_buffers). The cause is the Mamba-3 tap FIFO: step returns the FIFO as narrows of the FIFO tensors.
