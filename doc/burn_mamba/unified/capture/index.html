Each family’s CacheTensors traversal: a captured step writing its new cache in place. Graph-capture support for the caches: each family’s CacheTensors traversal, which is what lets a captured step (CapturedStep) write its new cache into the buffers of the old one.

One traversal per cache type, visiting every tensor field — optional ones must be present in both caches or in neither. Why the stable cache has to be copied into buffers of its own first (into_owned_buffers) is visible here: Mamba-3’s tap slots come back from step as narrows of its own tensors.
