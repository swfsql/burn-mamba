§Serial SSD with a custom, memory-efficient backward (Mamba-3 single-SSD)

The SerialRecalculated path of the single-SSD pathway. The forward is the same serial scan as super::super::serial, but it goes through the Mamba3SingleSsdBackendExt trait. So an Autodiff backend can use a custom backward that recomputes per-chunk intermediates instead of storing them (see super::backward / super::combined_backward). Unlike the double-SSD form, the kernels here apply the trapezoid gamma/scale themselves, so the backward also returns d_gamma/d_scale. (The caller folds the boundary-β seed into the initial accumulator.)

The default body runs under a generic backend B, where the high-level Tensor (pinned to Dispatch) is not available. So the K1–K5 math uses the rank-tagged [F] primitive wrapper. K1–K4 do not depend on the form, and come from the standard SSD scan. Only the forward K5 of single-SSD (strict-lower intra-chunk + γ-correction) is defined here. The backward of its LOWER and BLUE terms is in the kernels of that scan. The backward of its DIAG term is in super::diag.
