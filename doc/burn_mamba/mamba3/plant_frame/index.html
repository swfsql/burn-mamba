§Mamba-3 — Plant Frame

The plant frame is the work of a Mamba-3 block around the plant. Both forward pathways and step call it. Each one keeps only its middle: its scan and the inputs that only this scan needs. step enters at tokens = 1. It adds that read axis to its input and removes it from its output.

  input  ── plant_entry ──▶ rotated B and C, masses, tap FIFO
                                       │
                             the scan of the pathway
                                       │
  output ◀── plant_tail ───          readout
  • Mamba3::plant_entry holds the padding, the segments, the fresh cache, the origin, the in-projection, the split, the trapezoid coefficients, QK-Norm, the rotation and the tap FIFO.
  • Mamba3::plant_tail holds the positive systems, the D skip, the gate, the MIMO merge and out_proj. The band correction of single-SSD runs before the tail and stays in that pathway.

The frame has one cache type, Mamba3DoubleSsdCache. A forward starts and ends at a call boundary, where the accumulator of single-SSD equals the state. So the From impls convert the cache of that pathway (ADR-0003). The frame writes the frame cache (Mamba3FrameCache) of the cache. Only the scan of each pathway writes the state.

A bug in the frame fails both pathways alike, and a parity test cannot show it. So the tests of this module compare the split and the tail with their definitions (ADR-0007).
