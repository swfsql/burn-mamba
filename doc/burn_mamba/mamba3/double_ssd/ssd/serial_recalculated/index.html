Serial-over-chunks SSD with a custom recompute backward. Double-SSD serial scan with a custom, memory-efficient backward.

The forward and the Mamba3DoubleSsdBackendExt trait are in serial_recalculated. The registered autodiff node (backward) and the recompute-based gradient math (combined_backward) save training memory: they recompute intermediates instead of storing them.
