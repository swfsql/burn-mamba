Compute the trapezoidal discretisation coefficients from the raw (data-dependent) projections. See the header of mamba3.rs for the formulas and the header of tap_pattern.rs for how the masses divide.

All data tensors are [batch, len, nheads], where len is the write axis (forward) or the u micro-steps of one token (step). dt_bias_h broadcasts to match. In both cases, a tap that closes at token boundaries closes periodically along axis 1.

lambda_raw is None under TapPattern::None, whose block projects no λ channels. The coefficients then have their λ ≡ 1 values by construction: no mass is formed, and γ is Δ (the same tensor, not a new 1 · Δ). mu_raw is None unless the pattern has_two_taps. The same holds one level down: no μ, no second mass.

A tap closes by giving its mass back, not by dropping it. λ is 1 where the tap at tap_lag is not admissible (so γ takes all of the micro-step). μ is 0 where the lag-1 tap of a two-tap member is not admissible (so the lag-u tap takes all of the left endpoint). Both are exact at the ends, so the degenerate members equal their targets bit for bit.

gain is Some under a Kalman Gain. This function then computes the decay (crate::mamba3::positive::kalman::gain) before it forms α. So no consumer (the tap transports, the tap FIFO, the single-SSD key scale) can read the projected decay by mistake. The masses keep the projected Δ. The gain reads them in logs, formed from the same pre-activations (LogMasses).
