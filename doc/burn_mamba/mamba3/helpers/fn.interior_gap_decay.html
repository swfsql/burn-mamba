pub fn interior_gap_decay(da_bsh: Tensor<3>, lag: usize) -> Option<Tensor<3>>
Expand description

The interior of a lag-lag tap’s gap: Πᵈ⁼¹..ˡᵃᵍ⁻¹ αₚ₋ᵈ, or None at lag = 1 (where the gap has no interior).

A tap at lag L is transported across its own gap, Πᵈ⁼⁰..ᴸ⁻¹ αₚ₋ᵈ (info/trapezoid-as-integration.md §9). β = (1−λ)Δα already carries the d = 0 factor, so this is the rest of it.

The front is zero-padded, not clamped to what the call happens to hold: for p < L the missing factors are exactly the ones the cache’s v slots were already scaled by when they were stored (see tail_decay).
