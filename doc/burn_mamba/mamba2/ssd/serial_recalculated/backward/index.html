The RecomputeOp impl of the autodiff backend.

§RecomputeOp of the Mamba-2 SSD

Implements Mamba2BackendExt for Autodiff<B> with one recompute node. The node saves the inputs of the forward and not its large intermediates. The backward replays the inputs through combined_backward, which calls the standard SSD scan. This is the ~⅓ training-memory saving of the SerialRecalculated path.

Without resets, origin_bhpr is a placeholder with one element (packing::placeholder), which the node saves at no real cost.
